# The computed value of a color

A color written as a name, a hex number, `rgb()`, `hsl()`, `hwb()`, `lab()`, `lch()`, `oklab()`, `oklch()` or `color()` is its own computed value. A color that is
computed from other colors (`color-mix()`, a relative color such as `rgb(from red r g b)`, `alpha(from red / 0.5)`, `contrast-color()`) or from the environment
(`lab(calc(50 + sign(2cqw - 10px) * 10) 30 50)`) is computed here, when a style is computed (\ref format_css_computed "css-computed.md"), into the color the standard
names. Back to \ref format_css "CSS" and \ref text_format_references "Formats".

The conversions are written in `text` on the `math` library (`gmath_pow`, `gmath_cbrt`, `gmath_atan2`, `gmath_sin`, `gmath_cos`, `gmath_hypot`) and IEEE double
arithmetic. The C library's math functions are not used (`check-css-libm`), so a stylesheet means the same number on every host. The `color` library has no CSS
color spaces (no Lab, LCH, Oklab, Oklch, HSL, HWB, no interpolation) and `text` does not depend on it; `check-css-color-cross` compares the matrices of sRGB, Display
P3, Adobe RGB and Rec. 2020 (to a relative 1e-12), ProPhoto (to 1e-5 of the matrix: the draft gives its primaries to six digits, the standard and `color` to four),
the Bradford adaptation between D65 and D50 and the sRGB and Adobe RGB transfer functions with `color`'s, where it is installed.

## The spaces

`srgb`, `srgb-linear`, `display-p3`, `display-p3-linear`, `a98-rgb`, `prophoto-rgb`, `rec2020`, `xyz-d50`, `xyz` and `xyz-d65` (the same space), `hsl`, `hwb`, `lab`,
`lch`, `oklab` and `oklch`. Every conversion goes through XYZ with a Bradford adaptation to and from D50 (`lab`, `lch`, `prophoto-rgb`, `xyz-d50`); sRGB, HSL and HWB
are converted among themselves directly. The matrices are generated by `tools/css/gen-color-tables.py` from the chromaticities of the draft (and equal the matrices of its
sample code to 1e-13); `rec2020` has the pure power of 2.4 of the draft, not the piecewise curve of the standard it is named for. Out-of-gamut channels are kept at
every step: a computed color is not gamut mapped, which is a paint-time step (Color 4, 14).

## Missing components

`none` is carried as a flag beside the number, never as a NaN. A conversion keeps a channel missing where the target has an analogous channel (Color 4, 13.3: lightness,
chroma and saturation, hue, red and x, green and y, blue and z, `a`, `b`, alpha) or an analogous set that is wholly missing, and every other channel is computed with
the missing ones as zero. A hue that is powerless (hsl: S at most 0.001; lch: C at most 0.0015; oklch: C at most 0.000004; hwb: W + B at least 99.999) is missing in a
color a conversion made, and in a source before it is converted.

## `color-mix()`

The percentages are normalized (Values 5, 6.1), the colors are mixed pair by pair in order, premultiplied by alpha (a hue is not premultiplied) and with the hue method
(`shorter` by default, `longer`, `increasing`, `decreasing`); the alpha is multiplied by one minus the leftover (`color-mix(in lch, purple 30%, plum 30%)` has an alpha
of 0.6). The default space is `oklab`. A missing component takes the other color's value, and is missing in the result when it is missing in both. The result is written by
the mixing space: `color(srgb r g b)` for `srgb`, `hsl` and `hwb`, `color(xyz-d65 x y z)` for `xyz`, `lab(l a b)`, `lch(l c h)`, `oklab(l a b)`, `oklch(l c h)`, and
`color(<space> r g b)` for the others, with `hsl(h s l)` or `hwb(h w b)` kept when a channel is `none`. A mix that holds `currentcolor` is the mix as written (Color 5,
10.1), except in the `color` property, where `currentcolor` is the inherited color, and in a registered custom property.

```
color-mix(in srgb, peru 40%, palegoldenrod)   ->  color(srgb 0.8816 0.7545 0.4988)
color-mix(in lch, peru 40%, palegoldenrod)    ->  lch(79.725496 40.454249 84.763378)
```

The declared value is written with the percentages Color 5 ("serialized percentages") gives: none when every one is the same share, otherwise each, an omitted one as `(100% - specified sum) / omitted
count`. That is written as the section says even when it is negative: `color-mix(red 60%, green, blue 50%)` is `color-mix(red 60%, green -10%, blue 50%)` (Firefox 158.0.1 writes the same; Chromium 156.0.8078.47 does
not accept the form). Values 5's "normalize mix percentages" clamps the sum of the specified percentages to 100%, which would make it `green 0%`, and the web-platform-tests page for the form expects that; the two
drafts disagree, and the library follows the serialization section.

## Relative colors and `alpha()`

The origin is converted to the function's own space and its channels are the keywords (`r g b` from 0 to 255 in `rgb()`, `h s l`, `h w b`, `l a b`, `l c h`, `x y z`,
`alpha`). A `none` keyword is zero inside a calculation and stays `none` as a bare channel. An omitted alpha is the origin's. The result of `rgb()`, `hsl()` and `hwb()`
is `color(srgb r g b)`, the others keep their function; the channels are clamped as a literal is, except those of `color()` and of a relative `rgb()`. `alpha(from c / a)` is
`c` with the alpha replaced, in `c`'s space. A calculation that needs the environment (`cqw`, `ex`, `ch`) goes through the environment's callbacks; with none the color
stays as written, flagged.

## `contrast-color()`

White or black, whichever has the larger WCAG 2.1 contrast ratio against the color without its alpha (white on a tie). The draft leaves the algorithm to the user
agent and asks only for AA large text, which this meets for every color.
